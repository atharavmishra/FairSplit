Delete your FairSplit account

How to delete your FairSplit account and data, and what happens to it.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open FairSplit.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion happens straight away. If you signed in with Google a long time ago, Google may ask you to choose your account again to confirm it's you.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email [CONTACT_EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my FairSplit account". Send it from the Google account you used with FairSplit, so we can find your data. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Guest accounts are not linked to an email address, so we cannot find them by email. Delete a guest account from inside the app before uninstalling.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (anonymous ID, or Google name, email and photo) Deleted
Groups that only you had access to, with all their expenses and settlements Deleted
Your access to groups you share with other people Removed; you leave every group

What is kept, and why

In groups you share with others, your member name and the expenses and settlements you were part of stay in the group. Without them, everyone else's balances would change. The member is no longer linked to your account. The other members can edit or delete these entries at any time.

We keep no backups of deleted accounts beyond Google Firebase's own short-term recovery systems.
