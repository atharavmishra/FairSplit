FairSplit

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short: FairSplit stores only what it needs to split expenses with your groups. We show no ads, sell no data and use no tracking or analytics. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what the FairSplit app ("FairSplit", "we") collects, why, who can see it and how to delete it. It applies to the FairSplit Android and iOS apps.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
An anonymous account ID When you continue as a guest To keep your groups linked to you on this phone
Your name, email address and profile photo from Google Only if you sign in with Google or link a Google account To sign you in, keep your groups across phones and show your name
Group names and the names you give group members When you create or edit a group To show who is in each group
Expenses: description, amount, date, who paid and how it is split When you add or edit an expense To calculate balances
Settle-up records: who paid whom and how much When you record a payment To update balances
UPI IDs (optional) Only if you add one to a member To open your UPI app with the payee filled in

We do not collect your contacts, location, phone number, bank or card details, or your device's advertising ID.

Payments

FairSplit never handles money. "Pay via UPI" opens a UPI app you already have (such as Google Pay or PhonePe) with the amount filled in. The payment happens entirely in that app, under its own privacy policy. FairSplit does not see whether the payment succeeded; you record it yourself.

Who can see your data

  • People in your groups. Everyone who has joined a group can see its members, expenses and settlements. Anyone you give a group's invite code to can join it, so share codes only with people you trust.
  • Google Firebase. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • No one else. We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

How your data is protected

Data travels between the app and Firebase over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by Google. Security rules on the server allow only members of a group to read or change that group's data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use FairSplit. When you delete your account, we delete it as described on the account deletion page. Guest accounts that are never linked to Google stay until you delete them in the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any expense, member name or UPI ID in the app.
  • Delete your account and data in the app: Account → Delete account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by emailing [CONTACT_EMAIL].

Children

FairSplit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect, and mention it in the app's release notes.
