# Play Store listing

Copy each field into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.
Character limits are Play's; the counts were checked when this was written.

## App name (30 max)

```
FairSplit: Split Expenses
```

## Short description (80 max)

```
Split bills with friends for free. No limits, no ads. Settle up over UPI.
```

## Full description (4000 max)

```
FairSplit keeps track of shared costs for trips, flats, office lunches and anything else you pay for together, so nobody has to remember who owes whom.

It's completely free. There are no ads, no premium tier, and no limits on groups or expenses.

SPLIT ANY WAY YOU LIKE
• Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentages or by shares
• Amounts always add up to the rupee and paisa, with no rounding gaps
• Add, edit or delete expenses at any time

SEE WHO OWES WHOM
• Balances update the moment an expense is added
• Debts are simplified into the fewest payments, so a group settles up quickly

SETTLE UP OVER UPI
• Tap "Pay via UPI" to open Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app with the amount already filled in
• FairSplit never touches your money; the payment happens in your UPI app
• Record cash payments too

EASY FOR YOUR FRIENDS
• Start in seconds as a guest, no sign-up needed
• Invite friends with a group code; they join and claim their name
• Friends who haven't installed the app can still be added to a group by name
• Sign in with Google to keep your groups across phones

WORKS OFFLINE
Add expenses without a connection. They sync when you're back online.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Only people in a group can see that group. Delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Questions or ideas? Write to atharvmishra77@gmail.com.
```

## Before submitting

- Play's metadata policy bans naming other apps, ranking claims like "#1" or "best", and
  emoji-heavy text. The copy above avoids all three; keep it that way when editing.
- "Add up to the rupee and paisa" and "works offline" are true of the current build; re-check them if the
  split engine or Firestore persistence settings change.
